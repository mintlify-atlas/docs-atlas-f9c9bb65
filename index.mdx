LR2oraja Endless Dream is a drop-in replacement for [beatoraja](https://github.com/exch-bms2/beatoraja) — the open-source BMS rhythm game client. Built on top of [LR2oraja](https://github.com/wcko87/lr2oraja) (which restores LR2-style judges and gauges), Endless Dream adds a rich set of quality-of-life features, including an in-game mod menu, real-time skin editing, LR2IR leaderboards, and automatic song downloading.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Endless Dream">
    Grab the latest release for your operating system from the [releases page](https://github.com/seraxis/lr2oraja-endlessdream/releases).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install alongside beatoraja">
    Follow the [installation guide](/getting-started/installation) to set up Endless Dream in a new or existing beatoraja directory.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch and configure">
    Run the launcher and configure your BMS folders, audio settings, input, and skins before starting the game. See [First Run](/getting-started/first-run).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the mod menu in-game">
    Press **F5** or **Insert** to open the mod menu and explore rate modifiers, the random trainer, and skin configuration options.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Endless Dream requires **Java 17** (Full JDK with JavaFX). The recommended distribution is [Liberica JDK Full](https://bell-sw.com/pages/downloads/#jdk-17-lts). The bundled `beatoraja-0.8.8 JRE` download does **not** include JavaFX and cannot launch Endless Dream.
</Note>
